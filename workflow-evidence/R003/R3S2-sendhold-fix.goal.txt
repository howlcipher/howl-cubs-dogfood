Fix a counting defect in the existing uncommitted send/hold feasibility work in this repository (cubs_edge_lab/probe/sendhold.py, research/SENDHOLD_FEASIBILITY.md, research/sendhold_feasibility.json, tests). Use only the data already cached under data/; make no network requests.

Defect (found by the campaign controller's audit of the cached sample): opportunities are counted per runner movement segment, not per runner. In the Stats API play-by-play, one runner can have several movement segments on one play (for example 1B->3B and then 3B->score after a throw). Such a runner was counted twice, once as "held at 3B" and once as "sent safe": 40 duplicate records, 39 runners counted as both held and scored. Opportunities were reported as 109 (2025) and 114 (2026) instead of one per runner, and the held counts and league-wide extrapolations are inflated.

Requirements:
1. Count one opportunity per runner per qualifying play (runner on second at a single or on first at a double, fewer than two outs before the play). Classify each runner by its whole movement on that play: scored without stopping; scored after stopping at third on the same play (report separately as "advanced on a later event", since the send decision is ambiguous); out at home; held at third; out elsewhere; other.
2. Recompute and rewrite research/SENDHOLD_FEASIBILITY.md and research/sendhold_feasibility.json from the cached data, including the per-season table, identification rates, extrapolation and full-retrieval estimate. Acceptance check from the controller's independent recount of the same cached data: 183 qualifying runners in total (2025: 88, 2026: 95); runners whose movement includes scoring: 35 per season; out at home: 0; runners whose movement includes ending at third without scoring: 47 (2025) and 58 (2026). If your counts differ, explain the difference with examples rather than forcing a match.
3. Tests (offline, fixtures only, flake8 -j1 clean, no tracked-file writes, all existing tests pass, also in a clean clone without data/): a fixture with a multi-segment runner (1B->3B then 3B->score) counted once and classified as "advanced on a later event"; a runner held at third; a runner out at home with one out; a two-out play excluded; outs-before computed from the play rather than the post-play count.

Integrity: never invent or alter values; label FACT/INFERENCE/UNKNOWN; publish aggregates and at most 5 short examples.
