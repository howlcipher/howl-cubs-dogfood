Fixed definitions (do not change):
- Outcome for cohort Y: at least 50 MLB plate appearances or at least 20 MLB innings pitched in season Y+1 (2019 cohort: thresholds scaled by 60/162 for 2020). Secondary: any MLB appearance in Y+1. Outcomes come from the cached league-wide MLB (sportId 1) season statistics; a player absent from them had no MLB playing time.
- Primary segment: players with no MLB appearance in Y. Also report pitchers and hitters separately and the whole pool.
- Rankings: B0 (MLB PA+IP in Y desc, then highest level in Y, then younger); B1 (highest level, then OPS for hitters or K-BB% for pitchers at that level, then younger); B2 (logistic regression on MLB PA+IP in Y, highest level, age); P (persistence: positive iff the player met the outcome threshold in season Y); M (logistic regression on all existing features, standardization fitted on training cohorts only, L2 strength chosen once on the validation cohort's primary segment from {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10}).
- Training cohorts 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023; validation cohort 2024; rolling-origin test years 2021, 2022, 2023 (train on all earlier cohorts).
- Primary metric: top-k outcome-positive counts in the primary segment, k = 50 primary (fixed, from Cubs signing volume), 25 and 100 secondary; plus precision@k, AUROC, and a calibration table for M. Paired bootstrap (2,000 resamples, fixed seed) for M minus each baseline.
- Comparator for the later holdout: the best of B0/B1/B2/P on the validation cohort's primary-segment top-50 hits (ties broken in the order B2, B0, B1, P).
- Early stop: if the validation cohort's primary segment has fewer than 30 outcome-positive players, record early_stop = true and stop after writing the outputs.
